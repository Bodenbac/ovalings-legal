Campaign

6 islands with 10 stages each. Light and Shadow fight together here.

How the campaign works

  • Team up to 4, Light and Shadow mixed

  • 5 energy per battle

  • 3 waves per stage, the Alpha of the island waits on stage 10

  • Stars depend on the number of turns. A fast win brings 3 stars. 3 stars unlock Fast Forward for that stage.

The islands

  • 1 Sunny Meadow

    Enemies from the Light

    Egg in the chest at 30 stars: Normal Egg

    Rules of the Alpha

    • Immune to stun.

    • Heals 6 % every third turn.

  • 2 Misty Woods

    Enemies from the Shadow

    Egg in the chest at 30 stars: Normal Egg

    Rules of the Alpha

    • Immune to stun.

    • Immune to poison.

    • Below half health: enraged, speed +30 %.

  • 3 Crystal Caves

    Enemies from the Light

    Egg in the chest at 30 stars: Rare Egg

    Rules of the Alpha

    • Immune to stun.

    • Once below half health: shield of 20 %.

  • 4 Ash Fields

    Enemies from the Shadow

    Egg in the chest at 30 stars: Rare Egg

    Rules of the Alpha

    • Immune to stun.

    • Below a quarter of health: attack +25 %.

    • Immune to effects that lower his damage.

  • 5 Cloud City

    Enemies from the Light

    Egg in the chest at 30 stars: Epic Egg

    Rules of the Alpha

    • Immune to stun.

    • Below half health: defense +30 %.

    • Heals 6 % every third turn.

  • 6 Moon Abyss

    Enemies from the Shadow

    Egg in the chest at 30 stars: Legendary Egg

    Rules of the Alpha

    • Immune to stun.

    • Bloodlust: every time an Ovaling falls, 15 % more attack for good.

    • Below half health: enraged, speed +30 %.

Difficulties

Each difficulty opens after the last island of the one before.

Chests

At 10, 20 and 30 stars on an island one chest opens each. The chest at 30 stars holds an egg.

Island goals

A goal is reached when all 10 stages of an island have at least as many stars as the goal. Each goal brings one reward.

  • Goal 1 30 to 80Diamonds
    • Small XP Potion
  • Goal 2 60 to 160Diamonds
    • Fast Forward Token (3)
  • Goal 3 120 to 320Diamonds
    • Fast Forward Token (5)
    • Medium XP Potion

Diamonds, gold and items here apply to Normal. Higher difficulties bring more.

Loot

Every win brings gold. Eggs and XP potions drop rarely, and more often the further you get.

  • Normal Egg 2 % to 5 %
  • Rare Egg 0.2 % to 1 %
  • Epic Egg 0.01 % to 0.2 %
  • Legendary Egg 0 % to 0.02 %
  • Small XP Potion 3 % to 8 %
  • Medium XP Potion 0.2 % to 2 %
  • Large XP Potion 0.01 % to 0.3 %
