What's it about?

What's it about?

Ovalings is a collecting game: every egg hatches a monster that exists only once. Luck rolls its look, stats and free slots, and every Ovaling belongs to the Light or the Shadow family.

Light Shadow
  • 214base shapes for body, eyes, mouth and more
  • 40skin materials per family
  • 1 to 5eyes per Ovaling

How an Ovaling is made

Tap until it hatches

You get eggs in the shop, as loot and as gifts. Tap an egg 15 times and it cracks open to reveal your Ovaling.

Bosses of the dungeons

6 dungeons with 30 stages each, every one with its own boss and its own rules. From stage 30 the Ultra form waits, once a week.

  • Warden of Light Angel Tower Entry
  • Lord of Shadows Demon Tower Entry
  • Toad Queen Poison Swamp Easy
  • The Silent Monk Lake of Stillness Medium
  • The Sky Dragon Sky Path Hard
  • New The Shield Colossus Iron Bastion Hard

The campaign

Travel with your team across 6 islands, each with 10 stages of 3 waves. An Alpha waits on the last stage of every island.

  • 1Sunny Meadow
  • 2Misty Woods
  • 3Crystal Caves
  • 4Ash Fields
  • 5Cloud City
  • 6Moon Abyss
