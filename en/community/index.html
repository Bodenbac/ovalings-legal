Community

Ovalings is more fun together: events, leaderboards, the market, friends and chat.

Events

  • Daily and weekly events, often several at once. Each one appears 6 hours before it starts, and you join with your first point.
  • You compete in a group of at most 50 players with a similar level.
  • Rewards for points go straight to your inbox. Places 1 to 30 get a reward at the end.

Leaderboards

Lists by player level, Gene Score of your best Ovaling and the highest stage per dungeon. Worldwide or among friends, the top 100. Guests are not listed.

Market

  • From player level 10. Trading uses gold and diamonds, never energy. A fee of 10 % is taken from the price.
  • More market slots cost 200 diamonds each, up to 10.

Friends

Add friends with their friend code. Your showcase shows your best three Ovalings.

Chat

From player level 10, with one room for each of these languages: English and Deutsch. A filter checks every message, you can report and block. There are no private messages.

Inbox

Gifts from events, rewards and news arrive here.

Guests can play everything else. Market, friends and chat open once the account is secured.
