Questions and answers

What is Ovalings?

A monster collecting game for iOS and Android. Every egg hatches an Ovaling that exists only once, and with them you fight in the campaign and in dungeons.

When and where will it be released?

Ovalings is coming soon for iOS and Android at the same time.

Does it cost anything?

Ovalings is free to play. Purchases are voluntary, and the odds are shown right at the purchase.

Where do I find the ID of my Ovaling?

Open “Your Ovalings” and tap the Ovaling. Open “More info” and tap the card “ID, exists only once”. The ID is now copied.

What do others see when I share my ID?

Its look, stats, level, slots and profile. Never your account, your display name or the nickname of the Ovaling.

Why can I not find an Ovaling?

Check the ID for typos. Ovalings of an account that is scheduled for deletion cannot be viewed.

Can I trade Ovalings?

Yes, on the market from player level 10, for gold or diamonds.

How do I secure my guest account?

In the settings of the app, with your email address. Your Ovalings and progress stay.

How do I delete my account?

In the settings of the app, or without the app on the page Delete account.

Delete account

How old do I have to be?

At least 16. Under 18, purchases with real money need the approval of a parent or guardian.

Does this website set cookies?

No. Only your choice of language is kept in your browser.

How do I reach you?

Through the contact form or by email.
