Tip

Only one Ovaling fights. The boss throws eggs at it, and each recharge makes him stronger for good, so long battles get harder.

Abilities of the boss

Egg Barrage

one enemy · power 0.7 · 1 to 4 eggs, each at a random enemy · no cooldown

Effect
Target
one enemy
  • Throws 1 to 4 eggs, each at a random enemy, each a hit of 70 % attack. Thrown eggs are gone.

Recharge

self · only without eggs: all eggs back, 10 % more attack and health for good · no cooldown

Effect
Target
self
  • Only when no eggs are left: skips the attack, all 12 eggs come back and he gains 10 % more attack and health for the rest of the battle.
