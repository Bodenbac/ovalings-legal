Tip

In this dungeon you can bring exactly one Ovaling, from any family.

The boss throws eggs at your Ovaling, and each recharge makes him stronger for good, so long battles get harder.

Abilities of the boss

Egg Barrage

Effect
Target:
one enemy
Cooldown:
none
  • Throws 1 to 4 eggs, each at a random enemy, each a hit of 70 % attack. Thrown eggs are gone.

Recharge

Effect
Target:
self
Cooldown:
none
  • Only when no eggs are left: skips the attack, all 12 eggs come back and he gains 10 % more attack and health for the rest of the battle.

Passives of the boss

  • Immune to stun.
