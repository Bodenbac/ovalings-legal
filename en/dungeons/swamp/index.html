Tip

In this dungeon you can bring Ovalings from Light and Shadow together, up to 5 per battle.

The boss is immune to poison, and her own poison stacks up to three times. Cleanse removes it before she detonates it, and her spores weaken healing.

Abilities of the boss

Venom Tongue

Effect
Target:
one enemy
Cooldown:
none
  • Deals 120 % of attack as damage.
  • 60 % chance to poison the enemy for 2 turns (5 % of max health per turn).

Spore Rain

Effect
Target:
all enemies
Cooldown:
3 turns
  • Hits all enemies with 60 % attack.
  • 80 % chance to poison all enemies for 2 turns (5 % of max health per turn).
  • Gives all enemies Heal Reduction for 2 turns: healing is 50 % weaker.

Toxic Burst

Effect
Target:
all enemies
Cooldown:
4 turns from stage 13 turns from stage 102 turns from stage 20
  • Venom Release: every poison on all enemies deals all its remaining damage at once, then it is gone.

Passives of the boss

  • Immune to stun.

  • Immune to poison.

  • Her poison stacks up to three times.

  • Poison Blood: whoever hits her directly has a 25 % chance to be poisoned (3 % of max health per turn for 2 turns).
