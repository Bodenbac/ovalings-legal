Battle

Battles are turn based. You pick a move, or let the team fight on its own.

How a battle runs

  • Each fighter fills its turn meter by its Speed. Whoever fills it first acts next.

  • Crit chance decides how often a hit is critical, Crit damage how hard it hits.

  • With Auto the team fights on its own, and the speed button makes the battle run faster.

  • In the campaign, stages won with 3 stars can be skipped with a Fast Forward Token.

Effects

The strength of an effect always comes from the ability that applies it. Effects of the same kind do not stack: the stronger value and the longer duration apply. Buffs help your own team, debuffs hit the enemy. Tap an effect to learn more.

  • Shield

    Soaks up damage before health is lost.

    Effect

    A shield is a cushion made of a part of the highest health. Damage hits the shield first and only then the health. A shield has no duration and stays until it is used up. Every shield also reduces all damage taken by at least 5 % as long as something is left of it. Then the damage reduction icon shows.
