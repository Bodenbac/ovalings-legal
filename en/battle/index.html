Battle

How a battle runs

  • Each fighter fills its turn meter by its Speed. Whoever fills it first acts next.

  • Crit Chance decides how often a hit is critical, Crit Damage how hard it hits.

  • With Auto the team fights on its own, and the speed button makes the battle run faster.

Basic attacks

Slot 1 always holds the basic attack of the family. Both are equally strong.

Beam

Light
  • Deals 100 % of attack as damage.

Strike

Shadow
  • Deals 100 % of attack as damage.

Effects

Buffs help your team, debuffs weaken the enemy. Two effects of the same kind do not add up, the stronger one counts.

  • Shield

    Soaks up damage before HP is lost.

    A shield is a cushion made of a part of the highest HP. Damage hits the shield first and only then the HP. A shield has no duration and stays until it is used up. Every shield also reduces all damage taken by at least 5 % as long as something is left of it. Then the damage reduction icon shows.

    Every shield lowers damage taken by at least 5 % while it stands. All reductions together reach at most 50 %.
