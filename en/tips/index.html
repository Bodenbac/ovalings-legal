Tips and tricks

For new players

  • Collect Light and Shadow. In the Angel Tower only Shadow fights, in the Demon Tower only Light.

  • Win a dungeon, your first win surely brings a move.

  • You can clear the first stages in Angel Tower, Demon Tower and Poison Swamp with a single Ovaling.

  • Spend your energy before it is full. Full energy stops refilling.

  • Put new moves in on your Ovaling’s page.

  • Do the starter quests, they bring a Rare Egg.

  • Claim the calendar even if you missed days. Nothing expires.

  • Secure your guest account, or the calendar ends at day 6.

  • Give XP potions through your Ovaling’s level bar.

For experienced players

  • After stage 30, the Ultra form waits in every dungeon once a week. A win from Monday brings 80 diamonds and a move.

  • You only get passives here: Poison Swamp.

  • Win in few turns to get more stars. With 3 stars the fast battle opens.

  • Check the suggested price when you list. The market takes a 10 % fee.
