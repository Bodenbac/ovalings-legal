Permission for purchases in Ovalings

Players under 18 can only spend real money in Ovalings with the consent of a parent. Diamonds can buy eggs that hatch random Ovalings, the odds are shown in the game right at the purchase. You decide here whether purchases are allowed and how much may be spent per month.

Account:

Withdraw the permission for purchases. After that, purchases with real money are locked again.
