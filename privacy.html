Privacy policy

Last updated: 4 October 2026. This policy applies to the Ovalings app and the website with the legal texts. It tells you which data we process, why, and what rights you have. If the German and English versions differ, the German version applies.

1. Controller

  • Luis Dominic Bodenbach, Vogelsbergstraße 27, 60316 Frankfurt am Main, Germany
  • Email: [EMAIL]
  • Contact form: in the app under Settings, Legal, Contact, and on the website

We are not required to appoint a data protection officer. You can reach us about any privacy question through the channels above.

2. Which data we process

  • Account: email address, password (only stored as an encrypted check value, never in plain text) and year of birth.
  • Profile: display name and friend code.
  • Game progress: diamonds, gold, energy and their bookings, eggs, Ovalings with their history, items, backdrops.
  • Playing together: friendships, marketplace offers, inbox messages.
  • Contact form: your message, the topic, your email address and an encrypted check value of your IP address against spam.
  • Technical data: every connection to our server involves your IP address, the time and the request.
  • On your device: your sign-in (encrypted), your settings and cached images of your Ovalings.

We do not use analytics or tracking tools. The app does not access your location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone.

3. Purposes and legal bases

  • Account, game progress, friends, marketplace and inbox: so you can play Ovalings. The legal basis is the contract of use (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).
  • Year of birth: to enforce the minimum age of 16 and to lock purchases for players under 18 (Art. 6(1)(b) and (c) GDPR).
  • Confirmation emails and password reset emails: for your account (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).
  • Currency bookings and server logs: to detect fraud, abuse and errors and to run the game securely. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in a fair and secure game (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).
  • Contact form: to answer your request (Art. 6(1)(b) or (f) GDPR).
  • Deleting your account: to carry out your request (Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR together with Art. 17 GDPR).

4. Storage on your device

The app stores your sign-in, your settings (language, sound, appearance) and images of your Ovalings on your device. This is necessary for the app to work the way you want to use it (Section 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG). No consent is needed for this. If you delete the app, this data is gone.

5. Recipients

  • Supabase: our server runs at Supabase (Supabase Pte. Ltd.), data centre in Frankfurt am Main, Germany. Supabase processes the data only on our behalf and according to our instructions (processing under Art. 28 GDPR). Confirmation emails are also sent through it.
  • Other players: others can see your display name, your showcase, your backdrop and your marketplace offers. Only your friends can see your profile with your collection. Nobody can see your email address.
  • Apple and Google: they distribute the app and handle in-app purchases. They are responsible for this themselves and their privacy policies apply. We only receive the information we need to credit a purchase, no payment data.
  • GitHub: the website with the legal texts is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.). GitHub stores the IP address of every visit for security purposes.

We do not sell data and do not pass it on for advertising.

6. Transfers outside the EU

Supabase is based in Singapore and uses service providers, including Amazon Web Services in the USA. Your game data is stored in Frankfurt, but access from these countries is possible, for example for maintenance and support. The safeguard is the European Commission's standard contractual clauses (Art. 46(2)(c) GDPR). You can get a copy on request. GitHub transfers data to the USA on the basis of the adequacy decision for certified companies (EU-US Data Privacy Framework) and standard contractual clauses.

7. Advertising

Ovalings currently shows no ads. Before we introduce ads, we will update this policy. Personalised ads will then only be shown with your consent and only from age 16.

8. How long we keep data

  • Account and game progress: until you delete your account. We then mark it for deletion for 30 days. During this time others no longer see you, and you can cancel the deletion by signing in. After that we delete everything that belongs to your account, including your messages sent through the contact form, and your account ID and nicknames are removed from the history of Ovalings you sold. Guest accounts are deleted right away. Ovalings, eggs and items you sold to others belong to them and stay with them.
  • Contact form: until your request is resolved, then for at most one year.
  • Server logs: only briefly, according to Supabase's retention periods.
  • Records of purchases: as long as tax and commercial law require (up to ten years).

9. Your rights

You have the right of access (Art. 15 GDPR), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20) and to object to processing based on legitimate interest (Art. 21). You can withdraw any consent at any time with effect for the future. Just write to us through the contact form or by email.

You can also lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, for example in the country where you live (Art. 77 GDPR).

10. Children and young people

Ovalings is for players aged 16 and over. For players under 18, purchases with real money are locked until their parents unlock them. In-app purchases go through Apple and Google, where their family settings also apply.

11. Do you have to provide data?

For an account we need your email address, a password and your year of birth. Without them you cannot play Ovalings. Everything else is created while you play.

12. Automated decisions

What an egg contains is decided by chance on our server, using the odds shown in the shop. This is not a decision about you as a person. We do not create profiles about you.

13. Security

All connections are encrypted. Your sign-in is stored encrypted on your device. Values such as diamonds and Ovalings are only written by the server, and each account can only see its own data in the database.

14. Deleting your account

In the app: Settings, Account, Delete account. Without the app: use the contact form on the website with the topic Delete account. We will then delete your account with all its data, after a 30-day period in which you can change your mind (see section 8).

15. Changes

If the app or the law changes, we will update this policy. The current version is always available in the app and on the website.
