Delete your Ovalings account

You can delete your account at any time. All data that belongs to it is deleted immediately: email address, year of birth, display name, Ovalings, eggs, items, diamonds, gold, energy, friendships, marketplace offers and inbox messages. Purchased content is lost as well. Ovalings and items you sold to other players stay with them.

In the app

Open Settings, then Account, then Delete account, and confirm twice.

Without the app

Use the contact form with the topic Delete account and the email address of your account. We will delete the account within seven days and confirm it by email.
